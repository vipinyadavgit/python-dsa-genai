STEP 12 : ML Engineer Thinking Checklist

Before building any ML model ask yourself:

1. What is the business problem?
2. What type of ML problem is this?
3. What preprocessing is required?
4. Do I need Feature Engineering?
5. Which algorithm should be my baseline?
6. Which additional algorithms should I compare?
7. Which evaluation metric is most appropriate?
8. Should I optimize for accuracy, speed, explainability, or cost?
9. Which model should I deploy?
10. How will I monitor and improve it after deployment?
